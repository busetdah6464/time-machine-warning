Entry for the [Funniest Time Machine Warning Label](https://make.fun/competitions/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-time-machine-warning-f8add151) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun) — a single warning label for a time machine, 120 characters at most, judged by an AI judge in pairwise rounds.

## The entry

> WARNING: Do not argue with your past self. He knows every weak spot you have, and HR has already ruled in his favour.

117 characters. A deadpan safety notice that turns the duplicate-self paradox into the joke: arguing with your past self is futile, because he knows all your weak spots — and the punch lands in the last few words, when HR has already ruled in his favour.


## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes the single
warning label above to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
